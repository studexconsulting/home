DUO — Study financing

The Dutch government lends money to all students to cover living costs and tuition. We help you apply.

Huurtoeslag — Rent allowance

If your income is low, the State covers part of your monthly rent. We manage the application with you.

Zorgtoeslag — Healthcare allowance

Monthly subsidy to cover mandatory health insurance. Don't miss a single euro you're entitled to.

Life there

Living in
the Netherlands.

  • Everything by bike — Forget the metro. You cycle to work, class, the supermarket and the canals.
  • The weather — Yes, it rains, but it's never truly cold. With the right raincoat, it's perfectly liveable.
  • Student life — Introweken, societies, events. It's very easy to make international friends.
  • Real cost — Between rent, food and leisure, you can live comfortably on €900–1,200/month (and with grants, less).

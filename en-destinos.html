01 Before you choose

Research university or university of applied sciences.

There are two types of university in the Netherlands. Both award official degrees recognised across Europe, but their approach is different.

WO · Wetenschappelijk onderwijs

Research university

Academic education with a strong focus on theory and research. The natural route if you later want to do a research master's or a PhD.

Bachelor's
3 years (180 ECTS)
Focus
Academic and research-oriented
Afterwards
Direct entry to WO master's programmes
HBO · Hoger beroepsonderwijs

University of applied sciences

Universities of applied sciences (hogescholen) offer practical, career-focused education, with work placements and real projects built into the curriculum.

Bachelor's
4 years (240 ECTS)
Focus
Practical and professional
Afterwards
WO master's, often after a bridging pre-master

Research universities

Eight of the best-known research universities in the country.

AmsterdamResearch

University of Amsterdam UvA

One of Europe's most renowned universities, with a wide range of English-taught bachelor's and master's programmes and faculties spread across the historic city centre.

Known for Psychology, Communication Science, Economics, Law and Social Sciences

The city A busy social life all year round, festivals and a huge international community. Ideal if you want to be at the centre of it all; rents are among the highest in the country.

Ask us about the UvA

AmsterdamResearch

Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam VU

A strongly international university with a modern campus in the south of Amsterdam, next to the Zuidas business district.

Known for Business, Psychology, Medicine and Philosophy

The city Its own campus and a slightly calmer atmosphere than the UvA, 15 minutes by bike from the canals and terraces of the centre.

Ask us about the VU

DelftResearch · Technical

TU Delft TUD

One of the most prestigious technical universities in the world, a leader in engineering, architecture and design, with first-class facilities.

Known for Engineering, Architecture, Industrial Design, Aerospace and Computer Science

The city Small, quiet and with more bikes than people. Social life revolves around the campus and student associations, 15 minutes by train from Rotterdam and The Hague.

Ask us about TU Delft

RotterdamResearch

Erasmus University Rotterdam EUR

An international reference in economics and business: the Erasmus School of Economics and the Rotterdam School of Management are known worldwide.

Known for Economics, Business, Law and Medicine

The city Skyscrapers, bold architecture and a bar and club scene to rival Amsterdam's, with somewhat more affordable rents.

Ask us about Erasmus

Leiden · The HagueResearch

Leiden University UL

The oldest university in the Netherlands, founded in 1575. Renowned for Law, Humanities and Sciences, with a second campus in The Hague.

Known for Law, Humanities, International Relations, Archaeology and Sciences

The city Canals, 17th-century houses and faculties spread across the centre. Quiet during the week and lively thanks to its student associations.

Ask us about Leiden

UtrechtResearch

Utrecht University UU

One of the country's major research universities, with faculties in the historic centre and a large science campus, Utrecht Science Park.

Known for Medicine, Veterinary Medicine, Geosciences, Natural Sciences and Psychology

The city One of the cities with the highest share of students. Two-level canals lined with terraces, right in the centre of the country and youthful all year round.

Ask us about Utrecht

GroningenResearch

University of Groningen RUG

One of the most international universities in the country, with hundreds of English-taught programmes and lower living costs than in the west.

Known for Economics, Psychology, Pharmacy and Sciences

The city The student city par excellence: roughly one in four residents is a student. Lively nightlife and cheaper rents than in the Randstad.

Ask us about Groningen

MaastrichtResearch · PBL

Maastricht University UM

The most international university in the Netherlands: more than half of its students come from abroad. It teaches through problem-based learning (PBL) in small groups.

Known for European Law, Business, Medicine and Arts and Culture

The city Medieval streets, terraces and just a step from Belgium and Germany. A slower pace than the Randstad, ideal if you're after something different.

Ask us about Maastricht

02 The map

Everything is closer than it seems.

Amsterdam, Leiden, The Hague, Delft, Rotterdam and Utrecht are all less than an hour apart by train. Groningen and Maastricht are further away, with a character of their own and lower living costs.
